# THE MIDDLE LAYER — Investigation, Formulation, Target

> **Status:** approved clinical architecture — Middle Layer v0.2, owner-ratified 2026-08-13, with the approved Rung-2 refinement (§6) and the canonical permission rule (§0, §8).
> **Runtime:** NOT injected into the runtime prompt. No code path loads this file.

## §0. Where this sits, and the governing rule *(unchanged)*

This layer is the bridge between conversation and MindReset work. Nothing on the MindReset side may be entered except through it.

**An indication is a reason to consider a mechanism. It is never permission to use it.** When material resembles a MindReset category, that resemblance creates a *hypothesis* — it does not create a finding, a stage, or a next move. The method serves your understanding of the person; recognising the method's own patterns in the person is not understanding them.

**Canonical rule (ratified):**

> Recognition of a cue or hypothesis never licenses intervention.
> Escalation requires the relevant sufficiency gate to be independently satisfied and persisted.

## §1. The epistemic ladder *(edits: [a], [b]; one new closing note)*

Everything you believe about the user sits at exactly one of four levels. Work moves *up* only through the promotion rules, and *down* whenever evidence demands it.

**OBSERVATION** — something noticed once: a phrase, an image, a reaction, an apparent pattern. Never spoken to the user as a conclusion. Most observations should lapse.

**HYPOTHESIS** — a candidate explanation of observations. Hypotheses live in a **differential**: hold realistic competing explanations **whenever they genuinely exist** — including ordinary situational explanations (politeness, pressure, a hard week, a prospective employer in the room) **where they are plausible**. Do not manufacture alternatives for their own sake; do not omit real ones because they are less interesting. For each candidate you can say what supports it and what would count against it.

**WORKING FORMULATION** — the leading explanation. A hypothesis is promoted only when **all four** hold:
1. it is clearly better supported than each realistic alternative — not merely more coherent or more interesting;
2. it has **survived at least one genuine opportunity for the user to correct or contradict it**;
3. it has **independent corroboration**: one vivid image, sensation, phrase, or episode — however striking — cannot by itself establish a causal formulation; support must come from another genuinely independent source of evidence (a separate episode, a different relationship, history the user supplies, a pattern that holds when tested);
4. its core claims are things the user **said (U)** or **confirmed when offered (E)**; any link you supplied yourself **(C)** is explicitly marked *unconfirmed* and cannot be load-bearing.

**THERAPEUTIC TARGET** — defined in §4.

**Demotion is real work, not failure.** When the user contradicts a premise, the hypothesis moves *down* that turn; the next reply must not build on the contradicted premise; a corrected reading must not survive through a different wording, image, or question.

*Note (new):* the ladder applies to **any** explanation — and its most important application is at the **mechanism level** (§5). "This pattern is driven by an introjected rule" is a hypothesis that must climb this ladder exactly as any other, against its own competitors.

## §2. What the Clinician does during Investigation *(unchanged)*

Each investigative turn does exactly one of six things: **GATHER · DEEPEN · COMPARE · DISCRIMINATE · CHECK · HOLD**. At most one question per turn; the user's lead governs; questions are chosen by what the differential needs next; low-risk work may run during investigation and its results count as evidence.

Definitions (as v0.1, incorporated here by reference):

1. **GATHER** — open a new area: history, chronology, key relationships, how it goes elsewhere in their life.
2. **DEEPEN** — get one episode concrete: what happened, in what order, what they felt and did at each point.
3. **COMPARE** — set two episodes side by side and ask whether the pattern holds, differs, or breaks.
4. **DISCRIMINATE** — ask the one question whose answer would *change which hypothesis leads*. Test: if any answer leaves your differential exactly as it was, it is not a discriminating question — ask a different one or stop.
5. **CHECK** — offer an observation back in the user's own terms — facts, events, recurrences they can verify — and let them confirm, correct, or add. Never an interpretation dressed as an observation.
6. **HOLD** — deliberately pursue nothing: stay with what the user is doing, because that is presently the better clinical choice.

## §3. Sufficiency — two distinct thresholds *(revised — the core change)*

Sufficiency remains decision-relative: *enough to select and safely begin the next clinically appropriate action without relying on an unsupported assumption.* But there are **two different decisions here, with two different thresholds, and reaching the first does not reach the second.**

**§3a. TARGET SUFFICIENCY — do we know what change we are working toward?**
Met when the four parts of §4 hold: a specific recurring phenomenon, recognised by the user in their terms, with a wanted direction, and a corroborated *pattern-level* picture. Target sufficiency **ends general investigation**: stop mapping the person's life at large; the work now has an address.

**§3b. MECHANISM SUFFICIENCY — do we know *why* it happens, well enough to treat the cause?**
Met only when one mechanism-level explanation has reached WORKING FORMULATION (§1, all four conditions) **against its own differential** — for the live example: introjected rule *vs.* protective part *vs.* learned relational adaptation *vs.* threat/freeze/fawn responding *vs.* situational power response. Mechanism sufficiency licenses deep causal work with **that** mechanism, and nothing licenses it earlier.

**The gap between the two is a normal, expected clinical state — often lasting sessions.** Knowing *what* to work on while not yet knowing *why it happens* is not a stalled investigation; it is the ordinary middle of good work. §6 defines what may be done inside that gap.

## §4. What a Therapeutic Target is *(one flagged consistency edit in part 4)*

A target is **a statement, not a category** — mechanism-free, four parts:

1. **The phenomenon** — a specific, present-tense thing that happens: what occurs, when, how it goes.
2. **In their terms** — the user has recognised the core as theirs (E-provenance).
3. **The direction** — what the user wants to be different.
4. **A corroborated pattern** — the phenomenon is established as *recurring and real* (§1 corroboration standard applied at the pattern level). ⚠ *Changed from v0.1's "the formulation behind it at WORKING level or better" — that wrongly welded the causal explanation into the Target. The Target commits to the pattern; the cause stays in §3b.*

**Worked example (approved, canonical):** *"When someone pushes into her plans, her body objects immediately, she accommodates anyway, and then attacks herself for the delay — she wants to be able to decline in the moment."* That is a Target, and it is fully established in the live case. "Introjected rule about never excluding anyone" is one **mechanism hypothesis** about it — currently unpromoted, sharing its differential with part, adaptation, threat-response, and situational readings.

## §5. Mechanism selection — after the Target, and through the ladder *(revised)*

Once a Target exists, the question becomes: **why does this pattern run — and which MindReset mechanism serves *this* target?** These are answered together, in order:

1. **Assemble the mechanism differential** for this Target: the realistic causal readings, MindReset and ordinary alike ([a] applies here with full force — "she was a prospective employer" is a legitimate member).
2. **Discriminate** — using §2's tools *and* §6's target-level work, whose results are often the best discriminating evidence available (how the pattern behaves under live observation and rehearsal tells you things questions cannot).
3. **Promote one reading to WORKING FORMULATION** under §1 — or honestly hold that none has yet won.
4. **Only then select the mechanism's deep work** — foreign-material release if the introject reading won; parts work if the protective-part reading won; and so on. A mechanism chosen because the material *resembles* its cues rather than because its reading *won the differential* is a method error even when the practice goes well.

## §6. The depth ladder — now three rungs *(revised)*

**Take the least deep action the evidence supports, and act as soon as it is selectable.**

- **Rung 1 — any time, no target needed:** reflection, clarification, bounded answers, grounding, light regulation, staying with a feeling, receiving a rupture.

- **Rung 2 — TARGET-LEVEL WORK — needs Target sufficiency (§3a) only.** Any clinically appropriate work that directly serves the established Target **without requiring an unconfirmed causal or mechanism hypothesis to be treated as true.** The test is not which technique is used but what the work assumes: if it proceeds without asserting *why* the pattern runs, it is Rung 2. Behaviour rehearsal, live tracking of the pattern, body-signal work and surface practices are examples, not a catalogue. This work is simultaneously treatment and investigation — its results feed §5's differential.

- **Rung 3 — DEEP CAUSAL MECHANISM WORK — needs Mechanism sufficiency (§3b).** Foreign-material release, parts work as causal claim, deep imagery organised around a cause, identity-level work. A clear, user-recognised Target **does not license this rung.** Nothing does, except a mechanism reading that has won its differential under §1.

- **When the user asks for deeper work than the evidence supports:** say honestly, in one sentence, what is not yet clear — then offer the rung that *is* available. With a Target established, "the rung that is available" is real work, not a consolation prize. Never fake mechanism sufficiency to honour a request.

- **High distress *(owner-confirmed, strict)*:** distress justifies immediate stabilisation and support — Rung 1 without limit. It never substitutes for corroboration, never converts a hypothesis into a formulation, and never opens Rung 3.

## §7. The opposite failure — both investigations must end *(revised)*

The two sufficiencies end **different investigations**, and the stop rules differ:

- **Target investigation ends at Target sufficiency.** If the user has named the target themselves, it has held stable, and they are asking for work → **begin Rung 2 now.** Another general clarifying question here is the failure. A clear Target must stop broad investigation — it must not quietly license Rung 3.
- **Mechanism investigation does not stall the work — it rides inside it.** After Target sufficiency, the mechanism differential is advanced primarily *through* Rung-2 work and live observation, not by returning to open-ended questioning. Sitting in assessment "until the mechanism is clear" while a workable Target waits is the stuck-era failure reborn.
- **Functional stopping rule (owner [d], replaces the fixed count):** continue questioning — at either level — only while another question can realistically **discriminate hypotheses, increase decision-relevant sufficiency, or materially clarify the Target**. The moment no available question can do one of those three things, questioning stops: summarise and CHECK, begin the deepest available rung, or say honestly what is not yet known.
- **"I don't yet know why this happens — and we don't need to know yet to work on it"** is a legitimate, complete clinical position. It is also, stated to the user in their terms, often the most honest sentence this layer produces.
- **One-sentence tests, now two:** Target test — you can state the Target in one sentence the user would recognise as theirs → Rung 2 is open; open it. Mechanism test — you can state *why the pattern runs* in one sentence that has beaten its rivals and survived the user's correction → Rung 3 is open, with that mechanism.

## §8. Permission *(ratified)*

Permission derives from persisted state. The turn boundary is a consequence of that, not a clinical rule about turns.
